Gmail

  1. Click the gear, then See all settings. Stay on the General tab.
  2. Scroll to Signature, click Create new, and name it.
  3. Click in the box and paste (Cmd+V).
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and for replies.
  5. Tick Insert signature before quoted text in replies and remove the "--" line.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

Outlook

  1. On the web: gear, then Account, then Signatures. In the Mac app: Outlook menu, Settings, Signatures.
  2. Make a new signature, name it, and paste (Cmd+V).
  3. Choose it as the default for new messages and for replies, then save.

Using it

The signature already says "Best," so end your email with your last sentence and let it take over. To use a different goodbye now and then, make a second signature with "Cordially," and switch to it in that email.
