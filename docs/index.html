computational psychiatry · essays · The Young Man’s Game

I’m a senior in psychology at Baylor University, applying to PhD programs in computational psychiatry. I build mathematical models of how people learn from wins and losses, and I test them against the choices people actually make.

I also write essays on philosophy and literature, and I wrote a book of philosophical fiction, The Young Man’s Game. Its first two chapters will be posted here.

Now

  • Finishing a paper that introduces ORL + α, a reinforcement-learning model of the Iowa Gambling Task. More on the research →
  • Testing, with a preregistered plan, whether people switch between learning strategies partway through the task.
  • Writing essays, and posting the first two chapters of The Young Man’s Game. Read →

That’s the work, and the questions behind it.
