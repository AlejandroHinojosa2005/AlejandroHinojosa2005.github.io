Research Experience

Sep. 2026 – Present

Independent project: reinforcement-learning models of the Iowa Gambling Task

I taught myself R and Stan, implemented eight reinforcement-learning models of the Iowa Gambling Task from their published equations, and compared them on held-out choices from open data. A curvature extension of ORL that I introduced (ORL + α) predicted best, and on a new sample of 70 people it met all five predictions I wrote in advance. In a preregistered replication (N = 440), all ten predictions were met: a stacked mix of the models outpredicted the best single model (z = 12.5). I then showed that two published models share a mathematically identical term and combined them into a new model, NOVA. The mix weights NOVA most heavily, and that result replicated against predictions committed in advance. Latest results: alejandrohinojosa.com/research and github.com/AlejandroHinojosa2005/IGT-Modeling.

July 2026 – Present

Research Assistant · Social-Personality Research Laboratory, Baylor University, Waco, TX

I worked with Dr. Wade Rowatt on a study using Wave 1 of the Global Flourishing Study (N = 202,898) across 22 countries. I looked at whether a country’s dominant K–12 pedagogical tradition predicts two things schooling is often said to shape: adults’ sense of personal mastery and their approval of their nation’s government. I built a nine-category classification of countries from the comparative-education literature because none existed, preregistered three hypotheses on OSF, and ran every analysis in R, including a wild cluster bootstrap I implemented from the methods literature when no package supported our version of R.
